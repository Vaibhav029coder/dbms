Core Modules

Everything you need to manage
inventory

Five fully functional modules covering the complete inventory management lifecycle.

🏠
Live Dashboard
Real-time stats — total stock value, product count, low-stock alerts, and recent transactions at a glance.
📋
Product Management
Add, edit, delete and search products with category filters, supplier info, and unit price tracking.
🏷️
Categories
Organise products into custom categories with descriptions. Each category shows its product count automatically.
🔄
Stock Transactions
Record Receive and Issue transactions. Stock updates instantly and every transaction is logged with timestamps.
📊
Reports & Export
Low stock alerts, category-wise value bars, and a full inventory report. Export data as CSV with one click.
☁️
Cloud Database
Backed by Supabase PostgreSQL — a fully hosted, free-tier cloud database. Data persists across sessions and devices.
